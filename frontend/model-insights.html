Research Methodology & Limitations: This independent project uses historical English Premier League player data to train and evaluate Stacking Ensemble classifiers. Starter labels represent players who completed at least 60 minutes, while substitute labels correspond to 1–59 minutes played. These labels are retrospective proxies rather than verified matchday lineup records. Historical match statistics may include post-match information. Therefore, model scores should not be interpreted as calibrated probabilities, official starting lineups, confirmed substitutions, or reliable future-match forecasts. Reported evaluation metrics are derived from the training notebook.
